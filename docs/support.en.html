Your data

The squads you build and the photos you add are stored only on your device. There is no account, and we do not collect personal information such as your name or email address. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use for details.

Common questions

Move your data to another device: open the menu → Backup (export / import), export a file containing your squads and photos, then import it on the new device.

Can't find a player: try searching by surname only. Transfers and new signings are added regularly. If a player is still missing, you can add them yourself with “Create a player”.

Get the latest player data: close the app and open it again — the newest data is loaded automatically.

Delete everything: all data lives on your device only. On iOS, deleting the app removes your squads, photos and settings. In a browser, clear this site's data in your browser settings.
