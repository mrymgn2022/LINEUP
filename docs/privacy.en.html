Squad XI (“we,” “us,” or “our”) sets out below, in this Privacy Policy (this “Policy”), how we handle user information in the football squad-building app “Squad XI” (the “Service”).

In short: The data you create in the Service—teams, notes, photos, and so on—is, as a rule, stored only on your own device and is not sent to our servers. The only data sent externally is anonymous usage statistics (popularity data) used to improve the Service; it contains no information that identifies you personally, such as your name or contact details.

2. Information we collect and how we use it

(1) Information stored only on your device (not sent to our servers)

The Service stores the following information in your device’s local storage (such as your browser’s localStorage). This information is never transmitted over the network to us or to any third party.

  • Squads you create and save (formation, the players you place, second-choice players, bench, and so on)
  • Team names, per-player notes, markers such as injuries and cards, and captain settings
  • Player photos you add
  • Various display settings

When you use the backup export/import feature, this data is only written to or read from a file on your own device through your own actions; we are not involved.

(2) Anonymous usage statistics (popularity data)

To improve the quality of the Service and to provide certain features (such as compiling “popular players”), when you save a team we send the following information to our analytics server.

  • A randomly generated, anonymous device identifier (a random token that cannot identify you personally)
  • Information identifying the players included in that squad (player name and country code)

This information contains no personally identifying information whatsoever, such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or your device’s contacts or photos. It is used solely to compile, on an anonymous basis, which players are most often selected, in order to power rankings and improve the Service.

(3) Information recorded automatically when you access the Service

When the Service is displayed or communicates over the network, information such as your IP address, the date and time of access, and your environment (browser and OS type, etc.) may be recorded as standard access logs on our hosting providers’ servers. This information is used only to the extent necessary to prevent misuse and to operate the Service reliably.

4. Advertising

As of the effective date of this Policy, the Service does not display third-party advertising. If we introduce advertising in the future, we will revise this Policy beforehand and specify how the advertising providers handle information.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

The Service does not use cookies to track users. Settings and data are stored in your device’s local storage, as described above.

6. Retention period

  • Information stored on your device (Section 2(1)): retained until you delete it within the Service, delete the app, or clear your browser’s stored data. You can erase it yourself at any time.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (Section 2(2)): retained as statistical data for as long as necessary for compilation and analysis.

7. Security

We take reasonable security measures for the information we collect, including encryption in transit (HTTPS) and anonymization. However, because the data described in Section 2(1) is stored only on your device, we cannot be held responsible for its loss due to loss or failure of your device, erasure of data, or similar events. We recommend using the backup feature for important data.

8. Your rights

You can delete the information stored on your device at any time by using the Service or by clearing the app’s or browser’s data. Because the anonymous usage statistics are handled in a form that cannot identify individuals, they are, as a rule, not subject to individual requests for disclosure, correction, or deletion. If you have any questions or requests regarding this Policy, however, please contact us using the details below.

9. Use by minors

The Service does not actively collect personal information such as names from users. If you are a minor, please use the Service with the understanding of a parent or guardian.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may revise this Policy in response to changes in applicable law or in the Service. If we make a significant change, we will announce it by posting on this page. The revised Policy takes effect when it is posted on this page.
